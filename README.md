A dense one-page newspaper front page that sums up the world's news of
**Friday 20 November 2026** in a single 1024×1024 image: **48 stories**
across 5 regions, a UTC timeline strip, and an explicit date-check line.

The leads are Russian missiles landing in Poland — which Warsaw called a
deliberate provocation and NATO a grave violation — Wall Street sliding
into correction as AI shares plunge, Britain and the EU signing landmark
deals on food trade, carbon markets and youth mobility in Brussels, and
Russia's huge overnight raid on Kyiv's bridges. Around them: a researcher
claiming Claude helped prove the Riemann hypothesis, a breakthrough pill
for pancreatic cancer, the Palisades Fire arson verdict, a $7.25 billion
Bayer Roundup settlement, a 6.7 earthquake in central Peru, COP31 running
into overtime, and the IMF warning global debt is nearing 100% of GDP.
Every item was checked to have happened or been first reported on
20 November 2026 (UTC); news from 19 November or earlier was left out —
including the Shenzhen summit, the ICJ's Myanmar ruling, the Riyadh tower
strike and the Anthropic IPO, all of which belong to the 19th.

Every word on the page is drawn programmatically, so the text is crisp and
exact; the four illustration panels are AI-generated photographs (no text
in them) laid into the composition. The finished page was checked at full
size and at 300 px across for legibility, spelling and overlaps.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This copies `entry.png` to `out/entry.png`. It needs Node 22, no network,
no dependencies, and writes identical bytes on every run.
